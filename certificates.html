LABORATORY VERIFICATION

Tested by Accredited Independent Laboratories

All ammunition containers and specialized crates undergo environmental, mechanical, and pressure test regimes executed by Element Materials Technology and TEN-E Packaging Services.

Metallurgical Test

Tensile Weld Integrity Testing

Executed by Element Materials Technology. Destructive tensile testing verifies that resistance spot welds and seam welds on ammo cans exceed military tensile requirements, preventing catastrophic failure under rough handling.

REF: MIL-DTL-3060G § 4.5.3
Corrosion Resistance

ASTM B117 Salt Fog Exposure

Subjected to continuous 5% neutral salt spray fog testing to validate powder coat adhesion, epoxy primers, and zinc phosphate conversion coatings against marine atmosphere corrosion.

STD: ASTM B117 / MIL-STD-810G Method 509.5
Sealing Integrity

Vacuum & Pressure Leakage Testing

Submerged bubble leak detection and differential pressure drop validation to ensure 100% hermetic airtightness under extreme altitude and thermal cycling changes.

STD: MIL-STD-28687 / DEF-STAN 81-41
HAZMAT TRANSPORT QUALIFICATION

UN Performance Oriented Packaging

All munitions packaging supplied by Marine Defense Packaging has successfully undergone UN POP drop, stack, and vibration testing performed by TEN-E Packaging Services. Rest assured that any container supplied is fully certified under:

IMDG Code ICAO / IATA UN Model Regulations
  • IMDG Code — International Maritime Dangerous Goods
  • ICAO / IATA — Technical Instructions for the Safe Transport of Dangerous Goods by Air
  • UN Model Regulations — Transport of Dangerous Goods (4A Steel Boxes, 4D Plywood, 4G Fibreboard)
