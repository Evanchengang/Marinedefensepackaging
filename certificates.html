GOVERNING DEFENSE STANDARDS

Accredited Military Specifications

Our manufacturing lines are audited to ensure full compliance with the following international military standards.

UN HAZMAT CLASS 1

UN POP 4A Certification

Certified for shipping explosive ordnance, Class 1 hazmat munitions, and small arms ammunition per international UN transport safety regulations.

ISPM 15 COMPLIANT

IPPC ISPM 15 Heat Treatment

All wood packaging (155mm pallets, wirebound crates, timber boxes) is kiln-dried and heat-treated (HT 56°C / 30 min) with official IPPC phytosanitary stamps.

SAMPLING PLAN

MIL-STD-1916 & MIL-STD-406

Rigorous statistical process control, lot sampling plans, and visual inspection standards enforced across every production batch.

HERMETIC LEAK TESTED

1.5 PSI Submerged Leak Testing

100% of ammunition containers undergo submerged pressure differential bubble leak testing to guarantee hermetic water & vapor seals.
