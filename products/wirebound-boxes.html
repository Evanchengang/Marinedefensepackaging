Mission Application & Operational Overview

Marine Defense Packaging manufactures heavy-duty wirebound wooden crates engineered specifically to hold and protect metal ammunition cans (M2A1, M19A1, and custom ordnance containers) under extreme field logistics conditions. All wirebound packaging is produced in strict compliance with federal specification PPP-B-585D (Class 1, Class 2, and Class 3).

Wirebound boxes combine resilient, thin-veneer hardwood slats bound under high tension with continuous galvanized steel wires and heavy end cleats. This dynamic architecture flexes under severe drop impact and rough-terrain vehicular shock, absorbing kinetic energy without splitting or catastrophic structural failure. Available with or without wood preservative dip treatments to resist rot, fungal decay, and insect attack during extended outdoor depot storage.

Federal Specification Compliance & Markings

Manufactured using Group III and IV structural hardwoods. High-tensile galvanized wire binding and heavy wood cleating provide unmatched stacking strength and field durability. Stencilling, lot numbers, hazard symbols, and ammunition identification printing are executed entirely in-house per customer requirements.

Engineering Features & Defense Quality Standards

Engineered for high strength-to-weight performance, rapid field handling, and long-term depot storage.

IMPACT DISSIPATION

High Strength-to-Weight Resilience

The combination of thin-veneer hardwood slats bound under tension with continuous steel wires creates an energy-absorbing composite structure that outperforms rigid nailed wooden boxes in rough-handling drop tests.

KNOCKED-DOWN FREIGHT

Flat-Pack Knocked-Down Storage

Boxes are delivered flat in compact knocked-down mats (blankets). Reduces inbound transport and arsenal storage volume by up to 75%, assembling quickly on shop floors prior to ammunition container loading.

FIELD CLOSURE

Twisting Loop Wire Closures

Automated looping machines form precision matching wire loops on closure edges. Operators close and lock the box in seconds using simple hand twisting tools, ensuring tamper-evident, positive-lock security.

BIOLOGICAL PRESERVATION

Preservative Dip & ISPM 15

Treated with EPA-approved wood preservatives resisting mold, fungal rot, and wood-boring insects. Heat-treated in certified kilns with IPPC ISPM 15 stamps for unrestricted global military export.

Automated Manufacturing, Stenciling & Surge Readiness

High-volume automated stitching and assembly machinery ensures continuous supply for large-scale ammunition packaging contracts.

AUTOMATED STITCHING

Precision Wire Stitching Lines

Automated wirebound machines staple galvanized binding wire continuously across hardwood veneer slats at high cycle speeds, ensuring uniform tension and flawless staple clinch depth.

IN-HOUSE STENCILING

Mil-Std-129 Identification & Marking

Fully equipped in-house screen printing and automated ink-jet stenciling stations execute MIL-STD-129 ammunition markings, national stock numbers, lot identification, and hazard diamond placards.

QUALITY ASSURANCE

100% Dimensional & Drop Verification

Every production lot is inspected for wire gauge, staple retention, cleat squareness, and drop-tested per ASTM D5276 to guarantee field integrity before dispatch.

SURGE SCALE

Allied Arsenal Surge Capacity

Large inventories of kiln-dried lumber and high-tensile wire support multi-thousand box daily run rates, meeting urgent allied military surge demands without supply delays.
