PPP-B-585 Compliant Wirebound Crates

High quality PPP-B-585 compliant wirebound crates engineered specifically to hold M2A1 and M19A1 ammunition cans under extreme military logistics environments.

Technical Specifications
ENGINEERING PROFILE

Field-Proven Defense Hardware

Our wirebound crates for M2A1 and M19A1 ammunition cans are manufactured in strict accordance with federal specification PPP-B-585. Available with or without chemical preservative treatments to resist rot, fungal decay, and insect attack during extended outdoor depot storage.

Automated high-tensile wire binding and heavy wood cleating provide unmatched compression strength and impact resistance. Stencilling, lot marking, and ammunition identification printing are carried out entirely in-house according to customer requirements.

Quality & Delivery Commitments

  • Rigorous Quality Inspection — 100% verification against drawings, dimensional tolerances, and coating mil thickness.
  • Certificates of Conformity — All production lots shipped with full documentation and test certifications.
  • Rapid Production & Short Lead Times — High-volume manufacturing lines capable of scaling up for urgent surge requirements.
