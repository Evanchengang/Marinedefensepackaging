PPP-B-585 Military Wirebound Boxes

Engineered wooden transport boxes designed to protect military equipment and ammunition shipments during harsh intermodal transport.

Applications

Standardized packaging for small arms cartridges, artillery components, pyrotechnics, and general hardware requiring high strength-to-weight ratio containers.

Features

  • High Strength Wirebound Structure: Steel binding wires stapled across veneer/plywood slats for maximum tear and impact resistance.
  • PPP-B-585 Compliance: Engineered to federal specification PPP-B-585 Class 1 and Class 2 military requirements.
