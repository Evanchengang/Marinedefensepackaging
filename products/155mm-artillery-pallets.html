Reliable packaging solutions designed for secure storage and transportation of 155mm artillery rounds under demanding deployment conditions.

Applications

The 155mm Heavy Artillery Pallet is engineered specifically for secure vertical storage, intermodal handling, and tactical transport of 155mm explosive rounds. Designed to integrate directly with defense logistics infrastructure, automated handling systems, and field depot storage facilities.

  • Tactical Transport: Engineered for secure multi-modal transport via truck, rail, and sea container ships.
  • Depot Vertical Stacking: Rated for multi-tier vertical depot stacking with zero structural deflection.

Features

Fabricated from heavy-duty structural hardwood and steel hardware, engineered to meet demanding military drawing standards.

  • Vertical 8-Round Configuration: Precision-milled top and bottom retainers lock rounds securely in place.
  • Corrosion-Resistant Hardware: Heavy zinc-plated steel tie-rods and locknuts maintain clamping tension during rugged transit.
  • 4-Way Forklift Entry: Full perimeter 4-way access for standard forklifts and pallet jacks.
