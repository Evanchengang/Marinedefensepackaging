Mission Application & Operational Overview

As global 155mm artillery projectile production scales up significantly across allied nations to replenish strategic stockpiles, mission-critical packaging has emerged as a vital logistical priority. Marine Defense Packaging supplies heavy-duty 8-round vertical wooden pallet systems built strictly to US Army Technical Data Package (TDP) Drawing 12982865, MIL-DTL-70951 Type I, and NATO STANAG 4440.

The pallet architecture secures eight 155mm explosive projectiles in an upright 2 × 4 array. Precision multi-axis CNC routed upper retainer plates and heavy-duty structural baseboards isolate each shell, preventing projectile-to-projectile contact, safeguarding precision-machined copper rotating bands, and protecting delicate nose fuze threads during intermodal rail, rough-terrain truck, and containerized ocean transport.

Strategic Sourcing & Engineering Gate

Our manufacturing workflow operates build-to-print directly against customer-controlled engineering drawings. We perform thorough Design for Manufacturability (DFM) reviews to ensure full alignment with revision levels, specific base-bleed / boat-tail contours, electronic fuze shielding requirements, and end-user deployment criteria before production release.

Engineering Features & Quality Standards

Every 155mm pallet assembly undergoes rigid quality assurance protocols for dynamic safety, projectile preservation, and seamless integration with robotic filling facilities.

PRECISION MACHINING

±0.5 mm CNC Blind Hole Tolerances

Multi-axis CNC routing carves 8 top lifting-plug recesses and 8 base retention pockets within tight ±0.5 mm tolerances. Eliminates rotational slippage, locks the center of gravity, and prevents shell-to-shell impact during high-vibration tactical transit.

ERFB COMPATIBILITY

Stepped Relief for Base-Bleed Units

Modern extended-range munitions (ERFB-BB, Assegai) feature delicate drag-reduction base-bleed units that cannot support dynamic axial transit loads. Our baseboards integrate stepped counterbored relief to completely isolate the gas generator assembly.

ELECTROSTATIC CONTROL

Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) Shielding

Available with conductive surface treatments and anti-static elastomer cushioning pads maintaining surface resistivity between 106 and 109 Ω, dissipating triboelectric charges to protect electronic proximity fuzes and Precision Guidance Kits (PGK).

EXPORT LOGISTICS

Flat-Pack (Knocked-Down) Export Kits

Engineered for global defense supply chains, pallets can be delivered pre-assembled or as precision knocked-down flat-pack kits. Flat-pack shipping reduces ocean freight cube volume by up to 60%, ready for rapid assembly at projectile filling facilities.

PHYTOSANITARY

IPPC ISPM 15 & Kiln-Dried (≤ 16% MC)

100% heat-treated structural lumber bearing official IPPC heat-treatment stamps (core temp ≥ 56°C for ≥ 30 min). Kiln-dried to ≤ 16% moisture content, preventing fungal rot, mold growth, and chemical interactions with projectile paint or propellants.

DEPOT STORAGE

Integrated VCI Barrier Solutions

Optional heavy-duty MIL-PRF-131 Class 1 moisture barrier bags with integrated Volatile Corrosion Inhibitor (VCI) liner pads provide up to 20 years of strategic depot storage without degrading bare steel casings or brass rotating bands.

With 155mm shell production expanding worldwide, Marine Defense Packaging provides reliable manufacturing scale, controlled-drawing execution, and second-source surge capacity to allied defense programs.

Full build-to-print execution against customer-controlled Technical Data Packages (TDP). All multi-axis CNC routing programs and tooling fixtures are locked and revision-controlled, ensuring zero deviation across multi-thousand pallet contract lots.

SURGE READINESS

Second-Source Surge Capacity

Dedicated industrial wood packaging production lines with large raw timber inventories. Capable of scaling up to 300+ precision pallets per day to support allied arsenal ramp-up schedules and meet urgent delivery commitments.

QUALITY ASSURANCE

Lot Traceability & FAI Documentation

All contract shipments are accompanied by comprehensive Quality Records: First Article Inspection (FAI) reports, timber species and moisture certificates, mill test reports for strapping and hardware, and IPPC phytosanitary documentation.

REGULATORY COMPLIANCE

Pallet designs comply with international dangerous goods transport regulations: ADR (road), RID (rail), IMDG (maritime ocean freight), and ICAO/IATA (tactical airlift) for UN 0168 Class 1.1D explosive munitions.
