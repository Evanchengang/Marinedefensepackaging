ENGINEERING ADVANTAGES

Engineered to Solve 155mm Logistics Bottlenecks

Standard 8-round vertical configuration with precision CNC recesses (±0.5mm), ISPM 15 heat-treated C24 timber, ESD protection, and flat-pack shipping kits — covering M107, M795, ERFB-BT/BB, and Assegai projectiles.

155mm Pallet Specifications

  • Payload Capacity: 8 × 155mm Artillery Projectiles (2 × 4 upright array)
  • Nominal Footprint: ~720 mm × ~360 mm (standard half-NATO module)
  • Gross Loaded Pallet Weight: ~400 to 450 kg (live rounds) / ~330 to 370 kg (empty bodies)
  • Timber Grade: European C24 Coniferous Softwood (Bending Strength 24 MPa)
  • Strapping Requirement: 4 × High-tensile steel bands (≥19mm width)
  • Phytosanitary Standard: IPPC ISPM 15 Kiln-Dried Heat Treated (HT 56°C / 30 min)

Key Logistics & Production Benefits

  • Precision CNC Machining: Recesses cut to ±0.5mm ensuring automated robotic handling arm compatibility.
  • Base-Bleed Relief Geometry: Custom stepped bottom board cutouts accommodate extended-range base-bleed (ERFB-BB) units without fin damage.
  • Flat-Pack Ocean Freight Kits: Knocked-down (CKD) pallet components pack tightly, saving 60% ocean container volume.
  • VCI Corrosion Barrier Bags: Optional integrated Vapor Corrosion Inhibitor barrier foil bags for unpainted forged shell transport.
