PRODUCT OVERVIEW

Engineered to Solve Critical 155mm Logistic Bottlenecks

As global 155mm shell production scales up tenfold to meet allied defense requirements, packaging must ensure zero-damage transit, automated robotic handling compatibility, and complete phytosanitary compliance.

Standard 8-round vertical configuration with precision CNC recesses (±0.5mm), ISPM 15 heat-treated C24 timber, ESD protection, and flat-pack ocean shipping kits — covering M107, M795, ERFB-BT/BB, and Assegai projectiles.

Pallet Structural Specifications

  • Payload Capacity: 8 x 155mm Artillery Projectiles (2 × 4 upright array)
  • Nominal Footprint: ~720 mm × ~360 mm (or standard half-NATO module)
  • Assembled Pallet Tare Weight: ~35 to 45 kg
  • Gross Loaded Pallet Weight: ~400 to 450 kg (live rounds) / ~330 to 370 kg (empty bodies)
  • Timber Grade: European C24 Coniferous Softwood (Bending Strength 24 MPa)
  • Strapping Requirement: 4 × High-tensile steel bands (≥19mm width, ≥0.8mm thickness)
  • Handling Interface: 4-Way Forklift Entry & Standard Overhead Crane Rigging
MISSION-CRITICAL SPECIFICATIONS
PRECISION MACHINING

±0.5 mm CNC Blind Hole Tolerances

Precision multi-axis CNC routing carves 8 top lifting-plug recesses and 8 base retention pockets within ±0.5 mm tolerances. Prevents rotational slippage, protects delicate copper driving bands, and eliminates shell-to-shell impact during cross-country rail and ocean voyage.

ERFB COMPATIBILITY

Stepped Relief for Base-Bleed Units

Modern extended-range munitions (ERFB-BB, Assegai) feature fragile drag-reduction base-bleed units or boat-tail contours that cannot bear axial static or dynamic loads. Our base plate features engineered counterbored relief holes to isolate the base-bleed assembly.

STATIC DISCHARGE PROTECTION

Electrostatic Discharge (ESD) Shielding

Available with conductive surface treatments and anti-static elastomer cushioning pads maintaining surface resistivity between 106 and 109 Ω. Dissipates triboelectric charges to protect sensitive proximity electronic fuzes and precision guidance kits (PGK).

EXPORT SAVINGS

Flat-Pack (Knocked-Down) Export Kits

Supplied either fully assembled or as high-density knocked-down flat-pack kits. Pre-drilled, pre-cleated modules assemble in under 5 minutes at the filling plant, slashing ocean container freight volume and costs by up to 60%.

PHYTOSANITARY EXCELLENCE

IPPC ISPM 15 & Kiln-Dried (≤16% MC)

100% debarked solid softwood core-heat-treated to 56°C for ≥30 minutes bearing IPPC HT stamps. Strict kiln drying keeps moisture content strictly under 16%–18%, eliminating container condensation sweat and catastrophic thread corrosion.

CORROSION INHIBITION

Integrated VCI Barrier Solutions

For prime contractors exporting unpainted or ready-to-fill empty forged shells across the oceans, MDP provides integrated Vapor Corrosion Inhibitor (VCI) heavy barrier foil bags and desiccant arrays to guarantee 100% rust-free delivery.
