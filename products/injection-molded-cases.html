Injection Molded Tactical Firearms Cases

High-protection injection moulded cases ranging from cost-effective sidearm transport cases to IP67-rated crushproof tactical rifle cases with custom CNC machined foam.

Technical Specifications
ENGINEERING PROFILE

Field-Proven Defense Hardware

From a simple, cost-effective polymer case for commercial sidearms to a full-specification, IP67-rated tactical rifle case with custom CNC machined foam, we supply high-quality firearms cases tailored to any defense, law enforcement, or security requirement.

Features include ultra-high-impact structural polypropylene copolymer construction, automatic ambient pressure relief purge valves, stainless steel hinge pins, dual padlock protector hasps, and precision CNC laser/waterjet foam cutouts.

Quality & Delivery Commitments

  • Rigorous Quality Inspection — 100% verification against drawings, dimensional tolerances, and coating mil thickness.
  • Certificates of Conformity — All production lots shipped with full documentation and test certifications.
  • Rapid Production & Short Lead Times — High-volume manufacturing lines capable of scaling up for urgent surge requirements.
