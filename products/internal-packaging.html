Applications

Protection of sensitive guidance electronics, optical sensors, fuzes, detonators, and precision defense components against shock, vibration, and static electrical discharge during transit.

Features

  • Precision CNC Routered Foam: Closed-cell crosslinked polyethylene (XLPE) and polyurethane foam inserts routed to exact CAD tolerances.
  • Anti-Static & Flame Retardant Options: Material options compliant with MIL-PRF-26514 and ESD protective standards.
