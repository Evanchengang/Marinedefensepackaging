Engineering & Logistics Overview

PA117 and PA154 military fiber containers and extruded aluminum protective canisters for 60mm, 81mm, and 120mm mortar rounds with hermetic seals.

Governing Specifications & Design Criteria

  • Governing Standard: Built strictly to US Military Specifications (60/81/120MM, PA117 / PA154 SPEC).
  • Quality Assurance: Verified under MIL-STD-1916 sampling plans and DCMA origin inspection.
  • Environmental Protection: Engineered for extreme combat temperature, vibration, and moisture exposure.
  • Phytosanitary & Export Compliance: Fully ISPM 15 heat treated lumber and UN POP Class 1 certified.
