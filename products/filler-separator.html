Mission Application & Operational Overview

Filler and separator pads are essential tight-pack dunnage components installed between metal ammunition containers (M2A1, M19A1, PA108) inside military wooden crates and wirebound boxes. Marine Defense Packaging manufactures high-density solid fiberboard pads engineered strictly in accordance with military specification MIL-PRF-50449C and authorized ammunition crate technical drawings.

During harsh tactical transit and high-vibration maritime transport, ammunition cans can experience micro-shifting and severe metal-to-metal chafing. Precision die-cut solid fiberboard pads eliminate internal void space, dampen shock impulses, protect container CARC finishes and stencil markings, and maintain box rigidity to prevent strapping tension loss.

Built to MIL-PRF-50449C — Tested to Pass

Manufactured in accordance with MIL-PRF-50449C requirements: chemically neutral, non-reactive solid fiberboard, free of metallic inclusions. Inline metal detection is part of our QA process. Pads are tested to specification — water absorption ≤ 15% with no delamination after 48-hour immersion.

Engineering Features & Environmental Durability

Tested to MIL-PRF-50449C requirements to ensure performance under real-world depot and field conditions.

WATER RESISTANCE

Water Absorption ≤ 15%

Subjected to 48-hour continuous full immersion in water per military test protocols. Exhibits zero fiber swelling, zero ply separation, and retains over 85% of dry burst strength after soaking.

FLEXURAL DUCTILITY

180° Bidirectional Bend

Withstands full 180° reverse bending in both machine and cross-machine grain directions without fracturing, surface cracking along the crease, or delaminating the internal plies.

STIFFNESS & RIGIDITY

Drape Angle ≤ 40°

Evaluated using standard 6 × 18 inch cantilever overhang specimens. Outstanding board rigidity ensures pads remain perfectly flat during automated robotic or high-speed manual crate assembly.

EXTREME TEMPERATURES

+71°C (160°F) / 24h Thermal Stability

Conditioned at +71°C (+160°F) for 24 hours per MIL-PRF-50449C. No delamination, tackiness, blistering, or off-gassing in sealed crates.
