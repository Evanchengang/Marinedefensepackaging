Ammunition Fillers & Internal Separator Pads

Engineered HDPE divider grids, chipboard separator pads, and wood void fillers designed to maintain tight pack tightness inside military containers.

Applications

Preventing internal payload shifting, rattling, and pin damage inside M2A1, PA108, PA120, and custom wirebound container assemblies during military transportation.

Features

  • High-Density Polymer Grids: Die-cut high density polyethylene (HDPE) grids resistant to oil, moisture, and chemical exposure.
  • Engineered Wood Fillers: Precision-dimensioned wooden void fillers maintaining full pack tightness.
  • Die-Cut Chipboard Separators: Moisture-resistant chipboard layers isolating stacked ammunition rounds.
