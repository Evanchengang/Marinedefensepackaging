Mission Application & Operational Overview

Filler and separator pads are essential tight-pack dunnage components installed between metal ammunition containers (M2A1, M19A1, PA108) inside military wooden crates and wirebound boxes. Marine Defense Packaging manufactures high-density solid fiberboard pads engineered strictly in accordance with military specification MIL-PRF-50449 and authorized ammunition crate technical drawings.

During harsh tactical transit and high-vibration maritime transport, ammunition cans can experience micro-shifting and severe metal-to-metal chafing. Precision die-cut solid fiberboard pads eliminate internal void space, dampen shock impulses, protect container CARC finishes and stencil markings, and maintain box rigidity to prevent strapping tension loss.

100% Virgin Pulp Chemistry & Non-Corrosive Cleanliness

Fabricated exclusively from 100% virgin chemical wood pulp with strictly neutral pH (6.5–7.5). Absolutely free of recycled waste, sulfur compounds, corrosive chemicals, and conductive metal fragments, passing 100% inline industrial metal detection to protect brass ammunition and explosive components.

Engineering Features & Environmental Durability

Tested and certified against rigorous military environmental standards to ensure performance under real-world depot and field conditions.

WATER RESISTANCE

Water Absorption ≤ 15%

Subjected to 48-hour continuous full immersion in water per military test protocols. Exhibits zero fiber swelling, zero ply separation, and retains over 85% of dry burst strength after soaking.

FLEXURAL DUCTILITY

180° Bidirectional Bend

Withstands full 180° reverse bending in both machine and cross-machine grain directions without fracturing, surface cracking along the crease, or delaminating the internal plies.

STIFFNESS & RIGIDITY

Drape Angle ≤ 40°

Evaluated using standard 6 × 18 inch cantilever overhang specimens. Outstanding board rigidity ensures pads remain perfectly flat during automated robotic or high-speed manual crate assembly.

EXTREME TEMPERATURES

−29°C to +71°C Thermal Stability

Conditioned at +71°C (+160°F) and −29°C (−20°F) for 24 hours. Produces zero tacky exudation, zero surface blistering, and zero offensive odor or chemical off-gassing in sealed crates.

Precision Die-Cutting, Packaging & Surge Capacity

Delivering high-volume dunnage components with strict dimensional consistency directly to arsenal packing facilities and ammunition manufacturers.

PRECISION CONVERTING

High-Speed Hydraulic Die-Cutting

Automated steel-rule and CNC hydraulic cutting presses produce crisp, burr-free edges strictly within the negative tolerance limits required for effortless slotting into ammunition crates.

MOISTURE-SEALED BUNDLING
