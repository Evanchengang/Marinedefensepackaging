ENGINEERING PROFILE

Tight-Pack Dunnage for Ammunition Crates

Filler and separator pads sit between metal ammunition containers inside wooden crates and wirebound boxes. They take up void space, separate containers from each other and from box walls, and keep the pack tight for transport and marking.

  • Placed between metal containers inside M2A1 and M19A1 wooden crates
  • Side fillers for PA108 wirebound boxes
  • Additional end pieces sized for a tight pack at box ends
  • Die-cut to drawing dimensions — no on-site trimming

Material Specification

  • Substrate: High-density solid fiberboard (filler pad)
  • Reference spec: MIL-PRF-50449
  • Pulp: 100% virgin wood pulp — no recycled pulp; dense solid board, no delamination risk
  • Chemistry: pH neutral — non-corrosive, non-reactive
  • Cleanliness: free of metal impurities, oil stains, and conductive particles; passes metal detection
  • Surface: flat and clean — no blistering, wrinkling, or embedded foreign matter
  • Finish color (per crate drawings): lusterless, approx. forest green 34079 (FED-STD-595)
