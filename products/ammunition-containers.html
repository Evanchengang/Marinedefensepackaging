MANUFACTURING CREDENTIALS

Built Strictly to Military Technical Data Packages (TDP)

Every ammunition container leaving our facilities conforms to exact US Department of Defense (DoD) military specifications — with full drawing traceability across every production line.

  • MIL-DTL-3060G / MIL-C-70628 / MIL-C-70842 / MIL-C-70998 / MIL-S-50312 — built strictly to military Technical Data Packages
  • Traceability across stamping dies, automated continuous seam welding, zinc phosphating, and powder coating lines
  • Supplies national defense departments, NATO member armed forces, and global ammunition manufacturers — including CBC, Orbital ATK, General Dynamics, and European defense primes
  • Validated per MIL-STD-1916 sampling plans and MIL-STD-406 visual inspection; verified by Element Materials Technology & TEN-E Packaging Services
TT-C-490 Pretreatment MIL-STD-171 Finishing FED-STD-595 Olive Drab 34088 ASTM D-2000 Neoprene Gaskets

US-Canada Joint Certification (JCP)

ITAR / Export Controlled Defense Technical Data Access
  • Element Lab Tensile Welds — Hydrostatic and destructive mechanical weld tests confirm zero joint separation under severe combat vibration and drop impacts.
  • Neoprene Hermetic Seals (ASTM D-2000) — Continuous closed-cell elastomeric rubber gaskets clamped under cam-latch pressure guarantee water tightness even when fully submerged.
  • MIL-STD-171 Tactical Finishing — Heavy phosphate base with low-VOC infrared reflective (CARC) or alkyd enamel in FED-STD-595 Olive Drab (34088) or Camouflage Green.
