Applications

Heavy-gauge steel ammunition containers designed to protect small arms cartridges, medium caliber shells, fuzes, and pyrotechnics during transport and long-term depot storage.

Features

  • Hermetic Seal: High-grade rubber gasket lid seal preventing moisture and environmental contamination.
  • Heavy Steel Construction: Cold-rolled structural steel body with quick-release latching system.
