Technical Specifications
ENGINEERING PROFILE

Field-Proven Defense Hardware

We manufacture in-house custom-designed and fabricated timber and plywood crates and boxes for any military purpose. Our timber packaging plant consumes over 2,000 sheets of plywood every day, providing high-quality timber packaging for some of the world’s largest defense prime contractors and tech companies.

Equipped with automated panel saws, CNC routing stations, and heat-treatment kilns conforming to ISPM-15 regulations for worldwide export. We engineer bespoke internal saddles, shock-mounted skids, and heavy lashing hardware.

Quality & Delivery Commitments

  • Rigorous Quality Inspection — 100% verification against drawings, dimensional tolerances, and coating mil thickness.
  • Certificates of Conformity — All production lots shipped with full documentation and test certifications.
  • Rapid Production & Short Lead Times — High-volume manufacturing lines capable of scaling up for urgent surge requirements.
