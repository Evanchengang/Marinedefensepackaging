ENGINEERING PROFILE

Field-Proven Defense Hardware

Our engineering experience of designing and manufacturing bomb pallets dates back over 50 years. We manufacture the complete line of MHU steel pallets for the BLU MK82 (500 lb), MK83 (1,000 lb), and MK84 (2,000 lb) series of general purpose weapons.

Fabricated from high-tensile structural carbon steel with robotic welding, precision cradle radius machining, and heavy-duty tie-down lashing rings. Every pallet is treated with military anti-corrosive primers and topcoats for marine flight deck and munitions depot service.

Quality & Delivery Commitments

  • Rigorous Quality Inspection — 100% verification against drawings, dimensional tolerances, and coating mil thickness.
  • Certificates of Conformity — All production lots shipped with full documentation and test certifications.
  • Rapid Production & Short Lead Times — High-volume manufacturing lines capable of scaling up for urgent surge requirements.
