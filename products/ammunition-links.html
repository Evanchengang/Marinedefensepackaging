Applications

Bulk protective packaging for M13 (7.62mm), M27 (5.56mm), and M33 (.50 Cal) metallic disintegrating belt links supplying automatic weapons manufacturing lines and depot distribution.

Features

  • VCI Anti-Corrosion Protection: Vapor Corrosion Inhibitor (VCI) poly bags preventing rust during long-term storage.
  • Heavy Duty Outer Containers: Overpack corrugated/wirebound boxes rated for high density metallic link weights.
