NATO Approved Ammo Can Pallet Trays

The fastest, most convenient, and cost-effective method of palletising packed M2A1 cans. A full set of 5 trays palletises 96 x M2A1 cans in under 15 minutes.

Technical Specifications
ENGINEERING PROFILE

Field-Proven Defense Hardware

Our M2A1 patent-pending pallet trays offer a NATO-approved, cost-effective alternative to wirebound crates. They are the fastest and most cost-efficient method of palletising packed M2A1 cans for field delivery and storage.

  • 5-tray set palletises 96 × M2A1 cans — base tray, 3 intermediate interlocking trays, top cap tray
  • NATO qualified unit load — assembled by a single packing operative in under 15 minutes
  • Fits any can with the M2A1 footprint: M2A2, H83, PA19, PA60, PA70

Quality & Delivery Commitments

  • Rigorous Quality Inspection — 100% verification against drawings, dimensional tolerances, and coating mil thickness.
  • Certificates of Conformity — All production lots shipped with full documentation and test certifications.
  • Rapid Production & Short Lead Times — High-volume manufacturing lines capable of scaling up for urgent surge requirements.
