Ammunition Can Pallet Trays

Engineered wooden and composite pallet adapters designed for stable interlocking stacking of standard military ammunition containers.

Applications

Palletizing individual steel ammunition cans into rigid unit loads for distribution via military logistics networks, automated material handling equipment, and cargo aircraft.

Features

  • Precision Recessed Channels: Custom-milled grooves matching ammo can bottom rims to eliminate lateral movement.
  • Interlocking Tier Adapters: Interlayer wooden dividers enabling 3 to 5 tier palletized stacking under high strap tension.

Compatible ModelsM19A1, M2A1, PA108, PA120, M548 Container Layouts
