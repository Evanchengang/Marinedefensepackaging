REFERENCE TOOL

MIL-STD-2073 Packaging Code Reference

A quick breakdown of standard DoD packaging code fields found on government contracts and solicitations.

Method of Preservation

The process used to protect items against corrosion, moisture, or chemical deterioration (e.g. cleaning, drying, VCI bags).

Cleaning & Drying

The compulsory solvent cleaning and dry air process before applying protective compounds or barrier wrapping.

Preservative Compounds

Coatings or volatile corrosion inhibitors (VCI) applied to metal surfaces per MIL-PRF-16173 or MIL-D-3464 desiccants.

Cushioning & Thickness

Resilient polyethylene/polyurethane foam (MIL-P-26514) thickness calculated based on drop height and fragility.

Unit / Outer Container

The specification container level directly enclosing the item (e.g. M2A1 ammo can, PPP-B-585 crate, 155mm wooden pallet).

Special Markings (MIL-STD-129)

Required barcoding, NSN stencil marking, lot identification, and Hazmat Class 1 placards for depot acceptance.
