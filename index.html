SEC.02 — Engineering & Manufacturing

Built strictly to military technical data packages.

From initial prototype design to mass production runs, every container is subjected to non-destructive and destructive qualification testing.

  • 01

    Munitions-Grade Metallurgy & Welding

    Tensile weld integrity and airtight vacuum sealing tested on all metal ammunition containers.

  • 02

    Heavy Timber & Plywood Fabrication

    Over 2,000 sheets per day converted into PPP-B-585 crates, missile containers, and custom timber boxes.

  • 03

    UN POP Dangerous-Goods Testing

    All containers independently tested by TEN-E to meet IMDG, ICAO, and UN hazmat packaging standards.

  • 04

    Full Material Traceability

    Dedicated manufacturing lines in North America with full traceability from raw steel coils and alloy hardware to final powder coat curing.

SEC.04 — Procurement Process

From drawing to depot in four steps.

A disciplined procurement path for defense primes and government agencies.

Step 01

RFQ & Drawing Review

Submit your technical data package, MIL-STD requirements, quantities, and delivery window. Our engineers respond with a priced quotation.

Step 02

Engineering & Prototype

Build-to-print manufacturing or custom design support, with first-article samples for qualification trials.

Step 03

Production & QA

Volume production under MIL-STD-1916 sampling plans, with weld, salt fog, and airtightness testing by accredited labs.

Step 04

Delivery & Documentation

Export-compliant packing and multimodal delivery, every lot shipped with Certificates of Conformity.
