COMPANY PROFILE & HERITAGE

Over 40 Years of Defense Munitions Packaging Excellence

DEFENSE CONTRACT FULFILLMENT

Engineering Precision for Mission-Critical Logistics

From strict US Military Specifications (MIL-DTL-3060G, PPP-B-585, MIL-P-26514) to UK Defense Standards (DEF-STAN 81-41) and NATO STANAG requirements, our packaging systems guarantee 100% zero-damage transit across multimodal air, ocean, and tactical ground networks.

QUALITY CONTROL

100% Inspection Rigor

Every batch undergoes 100% submerged airtight leak testing (1.5 PSI), weld tensile testing, and salt fog corrosion spray testing per ASTM B117.

FLAGSHIP PRODUCT

155mm Artillery Systems

Standard 8-round vertical upright modular pallets for M107, M795, ERFB-BT/BB, and Assegai projectiles. Micron-level CNC tolerances (±0.5mm) and ISPM 15 lumber.

DCMA READY

DCMA Source Inspection

Full material traceability, MIL-STD-129 labeling, and DCMA origin inspection facilitation to ensure seamless DoD contract clearance.
