SEC.01 — Our Story

From Design to Production, At Your Side Every Step of the Way

Marine Defense Packaging provides total transit, storage, and packaging lifecycle solutions for munitions, explosives, and high-value military assets.

Founded on Precision Defense Supply Chain Partner
SEC.02 — Our Background

40+ Years of Defense Industry Partnership

We have been supplying packaging for over 40 years to some of the largest prime contractors and government agencies within the defense industry. Our in-depth knowledge and engineering expertise is what sets us apart from conventional commercial packaging suppliers.

From strict US Mil-Specs (such as MIL-DTL-3060G, PPP-B-585, MIL-P-26514) to UK Def Standards (DEF-STAN 81-41) and every ASTM standard in between, our packaging is manufactured and tested in rigorous accordance to the required technical specifications.

Marine Defense Packaging operates dedicated production lines consuming over 2,000 sheets of industrial plywood daily, automated wirebound stitching machinery, precision stamping presses, and robotic welding stations.

Our Core Defense Capabilities

  • 01

    Munitions-Grade Metallurgy & Welding

    Tensile weld integrity and airtight vacuum sealing tested on all metal ammunition containers.

  • 02

    Heavy Timber & Plywood Fabrication

    Over 2,000 sheets/day converted into PPP-B-585 crates, missile containers, and custom timber boxes.

  • 03

    UN POP Dangerous Goods Testing

    All containers independently tested by TEN-E to meet IMDG, ICAO, and UN Hazmat packaging standards.
